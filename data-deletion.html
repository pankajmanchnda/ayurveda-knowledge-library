Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Website Data

The website stores saved items, compare lists, and assessment history locally in your browser. To delete it, use the Clear buttons in the Research Workspace or clear this site’s browser storage.

WhatsApp Messages

To request deletion of message-related records controlled by the business, send a WhatsApp message or contact request stating: “Please delete my Ayurveda Knowledge Library WhatsApp data.” Include the WhatsApp number you used so the request can be identified.

Provider Records

Meta, Cloudflare, GitHub, and the configured AI provider may retain operational logs according to their own policies. Requests involving provider-controlled records may need to be directed to those providers.
