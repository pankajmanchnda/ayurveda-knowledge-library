Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Medical disclaimer: Ayurveda Knowledge Library is for educational research only. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. Consult a qualified physician or Ayurvedic practitioner before using herbs or formulations.

What This Service Is

Ayurveda Knowledge Library provides static educational reference content and a WhatsApp-based educational assistant grounded in the public knowledge library. The service is intended for general research and learning, not medical care.

Information We Process

If you use the website, the static GitHub Pages app does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information. Your browser may store local saved items or assessment history only on your device.

If you message the WhatsApp assistant, we process the WhatsApp message text, sender identifier, and message metadata needed to receive and respond through Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. Do not send sensitive medical records, identification documents, payment information, or emergency requests.

How Information Is Used

Messages are used to generate educational, safety-focused responses from the Ayurveda Knowledge Library. Messages may pass through Cloudflare Workers, Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, and the configured AI provider for response generation.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We use service providers only to operate the website, webhook, AI response flow, and WhatsApp delivery. These providers may process data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention

The website stores local workspace data only in your browser unless you clear it. The WhatsApp Worker is designed to respond to messages and does not intentionally maintain a customer database. Platform logs may be temporarily retained by hosting or messaging providers for security, debugging, and delivery operations.

Children, Pregnancy, and Emergencies

The service does not provide treatment protocols for children, pregnancy, serious disease, or urgent symptoms. If a message indicates red-flag symptoms, the assistant is designed to suppress herb suggestions and advise prompt medical care.

Your Choices

You may stop messaging the WhatsApp assistant at any time. You can clear local website history from the app workspace controls or your browser storage settings. For deletion requests, see the Data Deletion page.

Contact

For privacy or deletion requests, contact the business owner through the contact details listed in the associated Meta business account or WhatsApp business profile.
